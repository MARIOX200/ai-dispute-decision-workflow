# Service Not Provided - Internal Demo Policy

For disputes where a customer claims that a purchased service was not provided, determine whether there is evidence that the merchant fulfilled the agreed service.

Relevant evidence may include booking or reservation records, service-completion confirmation, customer attendance, access logs, signed acknowledgement, or correspondence confirming delivery of the service.

If credible evidence confirms that the service was provided as agreed, a representment recommendation may be prepared for human review.

If evidence is incomplete, contradictory, or does not clearly confirm service completion, escalate for human review and request service-completion evidence. If the dispute concerns service quality rather than whether the service was provided, escalate because a different policy may apply.
